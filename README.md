# Colon Cancer Survival Analysis

**An exploratory, reproducible R/Quarto analysis of overall survival in two colon-cancer treatment groups.** It compares Levamisole with Levamisole plus 5-Fluorouracil (5-FU), using Kaplan–Meier estimation, the log-rank test, and an unadjusted Cox model.

> **Read the analysis:** [Open the full Quarto report](https://github.com/ModelLearnRepeat/colon-survival-analysis/blob/main/analyse.qmd)

## Research question

How did overall survival differ between participants assigned to Levamisole and participants assigned to Levamisole plus 5-FU in the selected study data?

## Main results

The analysis includes **614 participants** in the two active-treatment groups and **284 observed deaths**. At approximately five years (1,825 days), the Kaplan–Meier estimates of overall survival are:

| Group | Estimated survival at 1,825 days |
|---|---:|
| Levamisole | 53.5% (95% CI: 47.8–58.9%) |
| Levamisole + 5-FU | 63.4% (95% CI: 57.7–68.5%) |

The estimated difference is **9.9 percentage points** in favour of the combination group. The global log-rank test gives *p* = 0.00417. The unadjusted Cox model estimates a hazard ratio of **0.711** (95% CI: 0.562–0.899) for Levamisole + 5-FU relative to Levamisole. Under the proportional-hazards assumption, this corresponds to an estimated 28.9% lower instantaneous death hazard among participants still alive at a given time. The proportional-hazards test found no evidence against that assumption (*p* = 0.737); this does not prove the assumption holds.

These are exploratory comparisons from the same data, not independent replications and not, by themselves, proof of a causal treatment effect.

## Analysis workflow

- Select death (`etype = 2`) as the endpoint; exclude the observation group from this two-arm comparison.
- Check the analysis population, event/censoring status, follow-up times, and required variables.
- Estimate survival curves and pointwise 95% confidence intervals with the Kaplan–Meier method; report estimates at one, three, and five years.
- Compare the survival distributions with the log-rank test.
- Estimate the treatment-group hazard ratio using an unadjusted Cox proportional-hazards model.
- Assess the proportional-hazards assumption using `cox.zph` and scaled Schoenfeld-residual plots.
- Explain censoring, risk sets, effect measures, uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations in the report.

## Data and scope

The analysis uses the `colon` dataset distributed with the R package [`survival`](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/survival/html/colon.html). The package data contain separate records for recurrence and death; this report selects the death endpoint and retains only the Levamisole and Levamisole-plus-5-FU groups. The observation group is not part of the research question addressed here.

## Limitations

- This is an **exploratory secondary analysis**; the Cox model is unadjusted.
- Kaplan–Meier and Cox interpretations rely on independent censoring within treatment groups. The analysis does not establish that assumption.
- The exact clinical time origin and individual reasons for censoring could not be conclusively verified from the sources checked. The report documents this source limitation.
- The proportional-hazards diagnostic found no statistical evidence against the assumption (*p* = 0.737), but a non-significant test does not prove it.
- The treatment groups, log-rank test, and Cox model all use the same dataset. The results do not address adverse effects, quality of life, costs, or current individual treatment decisions.
